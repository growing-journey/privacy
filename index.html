Privacy Policy

Growing Journey is a personal journaling application created by Dr. Eng. Lamice Denguir.

Data Collection

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

Local Storage

All information entered by the user (journal entries, photos, files, and notes) is stored locally on the user's device.

PIN Protection

The app allows users to protect access with a 4-digit PIN chosen by the user.

Children's Privacy

The application does not collect personal information from children and does not transmit data externally.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use analytics services, advertising networks, or third-party data processing services.
